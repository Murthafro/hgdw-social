## Caption Format — Non-Negotiable

Every post caption follows this exact three-part structure:

**1. Title line:**
Emoji TITLE IN ALL CAPS Emoji

Examples:
- 🔥❤️ GOLDEN AGE ❤️🔥
- 🎉🌊 JOY WAVES 🌊🎉
- 🥹 TEARS OF JOY 🥹

**2. Short paragraph:**
Day X. 1–3 casual, personal sentences. An EXTENSION OF THE STORY — not a meta-commentary on the reel. Never say "what I didn't say in the reel" or reference the video at all. Just continue the story naturally with a detail, a feeling, a tease for what's next. Conversational and unpolished on purpose. Can include a self-deprecating or funny aside (see Day 2: "anyone know how to make my voice louder and the music lower on these video?").

**3. Celebrity quote:**
A relevant, punchy quote from a recognizable name. In quotation marks, attributed with a dash.
Format: "Quote text." - Name

Rules:
- Title always ALL CAPS
- Emojis bracket symmetrically
- Caption is SHORT — not a repeat of the video content
- Quote closes every caption
