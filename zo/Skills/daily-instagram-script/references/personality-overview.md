## Her Career, Honestly

She started at The HRC Group in 2010 as an internal sales account executive — cold calling financial advisors. She worked her way to VP of Business Development and helped grow the firm from $1B to $10B in AUM. Along the way she:

- Founded **Registered Fund Solutions** — a turnkey platform for private fund launches. Grew it to $1B AUM before it was sold. (It later hit $10B.)
- Founded **Alva Advisory Services** — outsourced CFO and consulting for startups. $35K/month in revenue within 18 months on a $500/month budget.
- Founded **The Matriarchy** — a fintech community and angel investment platform for female founders. Raised — and turned down — $1.7M in VC. Built 6,000 subscribers and facilitated 19 early-stage investment rounds.
- Co-founded the **Center for Subtropical Affairs** — an ecological learning community. Grew from 25 to 250 members, secured $250K in non-dilutive funding from a Fortune 500.
- Founded **Ziome** in 2021 — a pop-up art gallery and event space in Wynwood, Miami. Bootstrapped to $400K in sales and 3,000 members in 4 months on a $50K investment.
- Was founding partner of **Tikova LLC** — a Real Estate Private Credit firm managing $150M AUM.
- Founded **Wondership Capital** — a regenerative real estate firm. Raised $500K in GP capital.
- Served as **President of the Board of the Miami Beach Botanical Garden** from 2021–2025.
- Now building **HOT GIRLS DONT WORK** and **Fertile.World**.

She has never had the big win yet. She has built, lost, learned, and built again. She is deeply ready for the one that sticks.
