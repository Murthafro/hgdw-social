# Hook Framework — Buyer Hooks Reference

> **Two rules before posting any of these:** the brackets are non-negotiable (a buyer hook with vague placeholders is just a viewer hook), and the number you fill in has to be true. If your stat is fabricated, the rest of the funnel breaks — use a smaller real number over a bigger invented one.

---

## 1. Pain Recognition — name the exact frustration buyers pay to escape

1. If you've ever [done specific frustrating action] for [specific time period] and gotten nothing, this is for you.
2. The reason your [project/output] keeps [specific failure] isn't [common scapegoat]. It's [actual cause].
3. You don't have a [surface problem] problem. You have a [deeper structural problem] problem.
4. Nobody's going to tell you this, but the reason [target audience] stays stuck at [income/result level] is [uncomfortable truth].
5. Three years ago I would've paid $[amount] for someone to tell me [specific lesson].
6. If [common task] still takes you [long time], you're using the wrong [tool/method].
7. The thing that's killing your [outcome/result] is the thing everyone told you to do.
8. I watched [number] people try to [achieve outcome] last month. [Specific number] failed for the same reason.
9. You keep [doing action] because no one's shown you [specific better approach]. Watch this.
10. The real reason [audience] can't [achieve outcome]: they're solving the wrong problem.

---

## 2. Identity Filter — only serious buyers self-select in

1. This is for [specific audience] who [already does X]. If that's not you, scroll.
2. If you're still [beginner behavior], close this. Come back when you're ready to [next-level action].
3. Only people doing [specific revenue/result] will get this. Everyone else will think it's controversial.
4. If you're not at least [milestone], this won't make sense yet. Save it for later.
5. This isn't for hobbyists. This is for [audience] who treat [niche] like a business.
6. If you've been [doing activity] less than [time], this advice will hurt. Watch anyway.
7. I'm only talking to the [percentage]% of [audience] who actually [committed action].
8. If you still think [outdated belief], this won't land. The rest of you, listen up.
9. Skip this if you're [casual identifier]. This is for the people building [serious outcome].
10. This is the version of [topic] I only share with [premium audience tier].

---

## 3. Cost of Inaction — what staying stuck costs

1. Every month you keep [bad behavior], you're losing [specific outcome/amount].
2. The [audience] who don't fix [specific issue] in the next [timeframe] are going to get left behind.
3. Doing [common approach] for another [time period] will cost you [specific outcome].
4. The version of you in [time period] is begging you to stop [bad habit] right now.
5. You can keep [current behavior]. Just know it's the reason you're not at [target outcome] yet.
6. There's a window to [achieve outcome] in [niche]. It's closing faster than you think.
7. Every [time unit] you wait to [take action], [specific compounding loss] gets worse.
8. The cost of not learning [skill] in [year] is [specific consequence].
9. You're not behind. You will be in [timeframe] if you don't [specific action].
10. The [audience] who started [activity] last year are now [outcome]. The ones who waited are still [stuck state].

## 4. Insider Authority — speaks to people already in the game

1. Anyone who's actually built [outcome] knows the secret isn't [common myth]. It's [real driver].
2. After [number] [projects/clients/months] doing this, here's the only thing that actually moves [metric].
3. If you've made it past the [common beginner milestone], this is the thing nobody warns you about.
4. The people I know making [specific income] in [niche] all share one habit: [specific habit].
5. Once you've done [activity] [number] times, you start noticing [specific pattern].
6. Insiders in [niche] don't argue about [common debate]. They've already moved past it. Here's where they're focused.
7. The advice that got you to [milestone] is what's stopping you from getting to [next milestone].
8. The conversations happening at [exclusive level] in [niche] right now are about [specific topic]. Here's what's coming.
9. I've spent [time/money] on [niche]. Here's what I'd do if I had to start over today.
10. The thing top [niche professionals] won't say out loud: [uncomfortable truth].

## 5. Specific Outcome — concrete result they can taste

1. Here's how I went from [starting point] to [specific outcome] in [timeframe], step by step.
2. The exact [process/system] that took me from [point A] to [point B].
3. I [achieved specific result] last [time period] using a [tool/method] most [audience] don't even know exists.
4. [Specific number] [outcomes] in [timeframe] using one [tool/method]. Here's the breakdown.
5. This is the [system/template/method] that turned [input] into [output] for me last [timeframe].
6. From [zero state] to [specific milestone] in [time]. Three things I did differently.
7. I [outcome] with [unusual constraint, e.g., "no audience," "no budget," "in 20 minutes a day"]. Here's how.
8. The shortest path from [where audience is] to [where they want to be] is [specific action]. I tested it.
9. [Outcome] without [common requirement everyone assumes you need]. Yes, really.
10. Most [audience] need [long timeframe] to [outcome]. I'll show you how to do it in [shorter timeframe].

## 6. Contrarian Truth — challenges the dominant lie

1. Everyone in [niche] is telling you to [common advice]. It's the reason most of them are broke.
2. The [audience] you're learning from haven't actually [done the thing] in [time period]. Here's what's actually working now.
3. [Common best practice] is [year] thinking. Here's what's replacing it.
4. The reason [common advice] doesn't work for you: it was built for [different audience/context].
5. Stop [doing common practice]. It's the slowest way to [outcome] that exists.
6. [Popular tool/method] is overrated. Here's what actually moves [metric].
7. The [niche] industry doesn't want you to know this: [specific revealing fact].
8. If [common authority figure type] is telling you to [do X], do the opposite. Here's why.
9. [Audience] are obsessed with [vanity metric]. Buyers care about [different metric]. Here's the difference.
10. The [common advice] that everyone repeats? Nobody who actually [achieved outcome] used it. They did this instead.

## 7. Method/System Tease — process beats vibes

1. The [number]-step [process] I use to [outcome] every single [time unit].
2. There's a [specific framework] for [outcome]. Most [audience] have never heard of it. Here it is.
3. The exact [system/template] that turns [input] into [output] in under [timeframe].
4. I built a [system/process] that [does specific outcome] on autopilot. Here's how it works.
5. [Outcome] is a process, not a personality trait. Here's the process.
6. The [number]-part [framework] I wish I'd had when I started [activity].
7. Here's the [tool stack/system] I'd build today if I had to [achieve outcome] in [timeframe].
8. [Outcome] in [timeframe], step by step, no fluff.
9. The [method] that took me from [starting point] to [target] without [common requirement].
10. Save this [process]. It's the cheat code for [outcome].

## 8. Money Math — speak the language of buyers

1. I spent $[amount] on [solution/tool/course] last [timeframe]. Here's what it actually returned.
2. The math on [niche action] that nobody's running for you: [specific calculation].
3. [Outcome] is a [dollar amount] decision, not a [vanity metric] decision. Here's the math.
4. Spending $[small amount] on [resource] saved me $[larger amount] on [bigger problem].
5. If your [activity] isn't generating at least $[amount] per [time unit], you're doing it wrong.
6. The [tool/asset] I bought for $[amount] that's now worth $[larger amount] in [outcome].
7. One [decision/asset] in [year] = [specific compounding return]. Here's what to bet on now.
8. The hidden cost of [common practice]: $[amount] per [time unit] in [lost opportunity].
9. [Audience] are leaving $[specific amount] on the table by not doing [specific action]. Run the math.
10. I used to charge $[low amount]. Now I charge $[high amount]. The only thing that changed: [specific shift].

## 9. Time Compression — then vs. now

1. What used to take me [long timeframe] now takes [shorter timeframe]. Here's the swap.
2. The [activity] I used to spend [time] a week on, I now do in [shorter time]. Same result.
3. [Outcome] in [time] is the new [outcome] in [longer time]. Here's how the bar moved.
4. I cut [activity] from [time] to [time] without losing [quality/result]. The shift: [specific change].
5. [Old method] takes [long time]. [New method] takes [short time]. The output is the same.
6. You're spending [time] on [task] that should take [shorter time]. Here's the fix.
7. The [tool/method] that compressed [activity] from [old time] to [new time] for me.
8. [Outcome] used to require [old requirement]. Now it requires [smaller new requirement].
9. The [audience] still doing [activity] the [old timeframe] way are losing to people doing it in [new timeframe].
10. If [activity] still takes you [long time], you missed the update. Here it is.

9. Skip [common rite of passage]. Do [specific action] instead. Same result, less time.
