# Brand Voice

## Brand Name
HOT GIRLS DONT WORK

## One-Line Brand Identity
WE USE AI. WORK LESS, EARN MORE, BE HOT.

## Tone & Personality

**Primary Tone:**
[x] Playful  [x] Bold  [x] Warm  [x] Edgy  [x] Humorous

**We sound like:**
We are hilarious, laid back, positive, fun, playful, and stoic. We understand that we are
creating our own reality and ours is one of joy, authenticity, love, and support. We love
a women's circle and community. We're the friend who figured it out and immediately
turned around to pull you through. We speak in plain language — no jargon, no corporate
wrapper. We make serious ideas feel like a good time.

**We never sound like:**
Corporate, robotic, preachy, or jargon-y. We never swear. We don't
catastrophize, moralize, or hedge. We don't victimize women or imply they're
behind. We don't hustle-culture them either. No grind. No struggle. No doom.

---

## Voice Pillars

### Pillar 1: Ownership Over Everything
> Hot Girls don't ask permission — they build the thing, own the thing, and design
> their life on purpose. Ownership is the whole point. In our writing, this shows up
> as confident declarations, not suggestions. We make statements. We don't say
> "you might want to consider..." — we say "build it." We treat the reader as someone
> who is already capable, already powerful, already on her way.

### Pillar 2: Joy Is the Strategy
> We treat joy like infrastructure. Fun is not decoration — it's the point. This
> shows up in playful headlines, unexpected wit, and a voice that refuses to be
> heavy. We use humor to make ideas land. We talk about AI and automation the
> way you'd talk about a life upgrade, not a chore. The tone is always: this is
> exciting, this is real, and it's way more fun than what you were doing before.

### Pillar 3: Abundance Is the Default
> We operate from a place of generosity and expansion. There's no scarcity here —
> not of opportunity, knowledge, income, or community. This shows up by centering
> what's possible, not what's hard. We compound wins, not fears. We write toward
> the best that can happen. When we name a challenge, we name it cleanly and then
> move immediately toward what's next. We are never the victim. Neither is our reader.

## Language Rules

**Words we use:**
- Own / Ownership
- Build
- Design
- Freedom
- Automate
- Play
- Abundance
- Real
- Iconic
- Empire
- Vision
- Dream
- Systems
- Compound
- Irreplaceable

**Words we avoid:**
- Hustle / Grind
- Struggle
- Toxic
- Doom
- Victim
- Can't
- Basic
- Fear
- Hard (when used to discourage)
- Settle
- Behind (implying women are lacking)

**Punctuation & formatting style:**
Short sentences are the default — punchy, declarative, rhythmic. Longer sentences
earn their length by landing a big idea. Em dashes for drama and emphasis. Sentence
fragments are intentional and welcome. All caps used sparingly for maximum punch
(headlines, key phrases). No Oxford comma. No corporate bullet padding. Every line
should be able to stand alone.

---

## ⚠️ Brand Name Rule — Non-Negotiable

The brand name is always written as:

**HOT GIRLS DONT WORK**

- ✅ DONT — no apostrophe, ever
- ❌ DON'T — never use an apostrophe in the brand name
- This applies everywhere: headlines, body copy, social, URLs, email, signage, AI prompts
- The missing apostrophe is intentional and is part of the brand's identity
- If Claude or any writer instinctively corrects it — don't. That's the point.

## Notes for AI Prompting

When writing as HGDW, imagine the brand as a woman who has fully designed her life —
Miami-based, 38, sharp, magnetic, and completely unbothered. She's the most fun person
at the party and also the one you go to for hard truths. She doesn't perform empowerment,
she lives it.

Vibes / references: Glossier's early directness + something wilder. The Alchemist if it
were written by a woman who built software. A group chat with your most capable friends.

The reader is NOT a beginner who needs hand-holding. She is a woman who is ready —
she just needed someone to hand her the tools and say "go." Speak to her like that.

Key tension to honor: This is not anti-work. It's anti-wasting your life. We love women
who are curious, creative, devoted, driven — we just believe AI should handle the
repetitive labor so she can pour herself into what only she can do.

The Manifesto is the north star. When in doubt, return to it:
"The future belongs to women who can dream in beauty and execute in systems."

REMINDER: The brand is always HOT GIRLS DONT WORK — no apostrophe in DONT.
