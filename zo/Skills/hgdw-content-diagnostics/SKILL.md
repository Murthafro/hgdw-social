## Step 3: Run the 3S Framework (in strict order)

### S1: STOP — Are you stopping the scroll?

Metric: **Skip rate** (% who swipe away in first 3 seconds)
- Compare YOUR skip rate to YOUR typical skip rate (not other creators)
- Goal: your skip rate < your typical skip rate

If skip rate is HIGH (bad):
- Fix the hook FIRST. Do not look at Stay or Share yet.
- Fixes: use symptom-first framing, add text overlay for muted viewers, try a different hook type from the library, lead with struggle not success
- Test: pair spoken hook with on-screen text

### S2: STAY — If they stopped, are they staying?

Only diagnose this AFTER Stop is healthy.

Metric: **Retention rate** = average watch time / total video length
- Judge on WEEKLY average, not daily
- If trending down → fix storytelling and pacing

Fixes:
- Use Problem-Pursuit-Payoff structure
- Add re-hooks (mystery boxes) throughout — new questions that keep viewer watching
- Check pacing: can you read each text line aloud before the next frame?
- Cut dead air, filler words, unnecessary pauses

### S3: SHARE — If they stayed, are they sharing?

Only diagnose this AFTER Stay is healthy.

Metric: **Shares per reach** (Instagram's head of product confirmed this is the #1 signal)
- A share = personal recommendation = primed viewer = retention + reach feedback loop

Fixes:
- Call out specific pain point + actionable solution
- Personal/specific hooks > generic hooks
- Make it DM-worthy: "I need to send this to [friend]"
- Check Instagram Edits app for share-rate comparison
