## The 4 Missions

Every post serves exactly ONE mission:

| Mission | Job | What it builds |
|---|---|---|
| **Attract** | Get attention, bring new people in | Reach |
| **Nurture** | Build trust, deepen connection | Relationship |
| **Position** | Establish authority and expertise | Credibility |
| **Convert** | Turn attention into revenue | Revenue |

## The 3 Pillars

Every post lives in ONE pillar:

| Pillar | What it is | What it builds |
|---|---|---|
| **Skill** | What you know — AI, automation, building | Credibility + monetization anchor |
| **Passion** | What you love — lifestyle, community, fun | Likeability, rapport |
| **Story** | Who you are — transformations, struggles | Trust (fastest-growing) |

## Weekly Mix (7 posts/week)

### By Mission:
- Attract: 3-4 posts
- Nurture: 1-2 posts
- Position: 1-2 posts
- Convert: 0-1 posts

### By Pillar:
- Skill: ~4 posts
- Story: ~2 posts
- Passion: ~1 post

### By Format:
- Direct to camera (talking head): 2-3
- Carousel: 1-2
- B-roll + text caption (silent film): 2-3

## Step 0: Check Story Continuity

## Step 3: Generate Content Ideas (3-5 per mission)

For each idea, specify:
- **Hook** (use symptom-first framing — patient language, not doctor language)
- **Mission** (A/N/P/C)
- **Pillar** (Skill/Passion/Story)
- **Format** (talking head / carousel / b-roll+caption)
- **Two-layer story** (surface topic + emotional truth underneath — the Mystery Box principle)

### Attract ideas (struggle-led story, or trending format + HGDW POV):
- Lead with struggle, not success
- Use "I" framing, not "you" framing
- Trending format + our message > inventing new formats

### Nurture ideas (current struggle, behind-the-scenes, not-yet-resolved):
- Show the raw. The mid-pursuit version.
- Goal: "this sounds exactly like me" reactions

### Position ideas (direct teaching, specific wins, results):
- Demonstrate expertise through teaching, not claiming
- Talk about wins regularly, not just once

### Convert ideas (proof + clear CTA):
- Reuse formats that already worked in A/N/P
- End with ONE clear next step

## Step 4: Draft Hooks (2-3 options per post)

Use the hook library. For each hook:
- Written (on-screen text for muted viewers)
- Spoken (what to say out loud)
- Apply the 3-second rule: would this stop YOUR scroll?

Hook quality checks:
- Symptom-first, not diagnosis-first
- Personal/specific, not generic
- Creates a Mystery Box (opens a question, doesn't answer it immediately)

## Step 5: Run Diagnostic Pass

Before finalizing, check each draft against:

| Signal | Bad | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Self-centered | Audience-first |
| Structure | Unstructured, vague | Structured, clear, repeatable |
| Value | Generic advice | High, specific, meaningful |
| Opening | Easy to scroll past | Strong hook, takes viewer on journey |
