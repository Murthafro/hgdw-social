# Prompt: Meeting Prep

> Walk into any meeting sharp, prepared, and ready to lead.

---

## How to Use
1. Fill in the meeting details below
2. Paste any relevant context (prior emails, notes, proposals)
3. Run it and walk in ready

---

## The Prompt

```
I have an upcoming meeting and I need to prepare. Help me walk in sharp.

MEETING DETAILS:
- Type: [E.g. sales call, partnership discussion, team standup, investor conversation, client check-in]
- Who I'm meeting with: [Name, title, company — and any relevant background you have on them]
- Duration: [How long]
- Format: [Video call / in person / phone]
- My goal for this meeting: [What does success look like when I hang up?]

CONTEXT:
[Paste any relevant background — prior emails, their website, a proposal, notes from last time you spoke, etc.]

PLEASE PREPARE:

1. **Quick intel brief** — 3–5 bullet points on who I'm meeting with and what they likely care about

2. **My agenda** — A suggested structure for the meeting with time allocations

3. **Opening statement** — How I should open the call (30–60 seconds, confident and clear)

4. **Key questions to ask** — 5 questions that will give me the most useful information

5. **Objections to anticipate** — 3 likely pushbacks and how I should handle them

6. **My ask / close** — How to end the meeting with a clear next step

7. **Red flags to watch for** — Anything I should pay attention to based on the context

Keep it tight. I need to be able to scan this in 5 minutes before the call.
```
