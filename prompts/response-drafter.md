# Prompt: Response Drafter

> Draft any professional response — email, DM, comment, or message — in your voice.

---

## How to Use
1. Paste the message you need to respond to
2. Fill in the context fields
3. Get 2–3 draft options to choose from or remix

---

## The Prompt

```
Help me draft a response to the following message. Match my communication style: [DESCRIBE YOUR STYLE — e.g. "direct but warm, no corporate-speak, I don't over-explain, I use short sentences"]

---

THE MESSAGE I RECEIVED:
[Paste the full message, email, DM, or comment here]

---

CONTEXT:
- Who sent this: [Name, role, relationship to you — e.g. "potential customer," "existing client," "press inquiry," "cold pitch"]
- Platform / format: [E.g. email, Instagram DM, LinkedIn message, community comment]
- What I want to accomplish: [E.g. "decline politely," "get more info," "close the deal," "set a boundary," "express gratitude without over-committing"]
- Anything I definitely want to include: [Key points, offers, links, asks]
- Anything I definitely do NOT want to include: [Off-limits topics, things I don't want to promise]
- Tone for this specific response: [E.g. "warmer than usual," "firm but kind," "excited," "professional and brief"]

---

GIVE ME:
1. **Draft A** — [First approach — describe the angle]
2. **Draft B** — [Second approach — slightly different tone or strategy]
3. **Draft C** — [Optional third angle, if there's a meaningfully different way to handle this]

After the drafts, add one line noting which you'd recommend and why.

Keep all drafts tight. No filler. No unnecessary openers like "I hope this finds you well."
```
