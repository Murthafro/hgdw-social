# Prompt: Weekly Report

> Turn your raw week into a clean, strategic summary in one pass.

---

## How to Use
1. Do a brain dump of your week in the `[BRAIN DUMP]` section — messy is fine
2. Fill in your metrics and blockers
3. Run it to get a polished report you can share or save

---

## The Prompt

```
You are helping me generate a weekly business report. Transform my raw notes into a clean, strategic summary.

WEEK OF: [Date range]

BRAIN DUMP — What happened this week (messy is fine):
[Just write everything — wins, losses, conversations, tasks completed, tasks dropped, random thoughts — stream of consciousness is perfect]

METRICS THIS WEEK:
- Revenue: [$ amount or "N/A"]
- New leads / sign-ups: [Number]
- Active customers: [Number]
- Key metric I track: [Metric name] = [Value]
- Other: [Any other number that matters]

CURRENT BLOCKERS:
[What's slowing you down or stuck]

TOP PRIORITY NEXT WEEK:
[What is the ONE most important thing to accomplish]

---

FORMAT THE REPORT AS:

## Week of [Date] — Weekly Report

**🏆 Wins**
[3–5 bullet points, specific and concrete]

**📊 Numbers**
[Clean metrics table]

**⚠️ Challenges**
[Honest assessment, no spin]

**🔍 Insights**
[Patterns or learnings from this week — what would you tell your future self?]

**➡️ Next Week: Top Priorities**
[Ranked list, max 5 items]

**🚧 Needs Attention**
[Things that are at risk or need a decision]

Keep it honest, clear, and useful — not performative.
```
