## My Sentence Patterns

- **Opens with curiosity or wonder** — a question, a vision, an observation about how the world works
- **Mixes the cosmic with the casual** — goes from "the universe is a matrix" to "chatty gapattie" without missing a beat
- **Short punchy declarations** — often capitalized for emphasis: "CURRENTS ARE CHANNELIZED TRANSFORMATION."
- **Poetic lists** — uses parallel structure to build rhythm: *"The breath of form, the gesture of time, the pulse of life."*
- **First-person and honest** — shares personal experience openly: *"I have always felt prone to getting lost in the sauce of my thoughts."*
- **Closes with warmth and lightness** — endings feel like a hug, not a hard sell: *"I hope you enjoy the musings while they last. XO COURT"*
- **Validates before she invites** — meets people exactly where they are before ever pitching anything: *"I see you. I was there."*
- **Reframes instead of hyping** — takes the fear or FOMO out and replaces it with possibility: *"You're not behind. The game just started."*
- **Goes specific, not general** — real details, not vague inspiration: month in January, 47 tabs, a friend who taught coding since middle school. Specificity = trust.
- **Invitation, never hard sell** — the close is always warm and casual, never urgent or pushy: *"Come through."*

---

## Writing Rules

- **Sentence length:** Mix of short, punchy declarations and longer lyrical sentences. Short for impact. Long for poetry and depth. Never rambling.
- **Vocabulary level:** Plain, accessible language. If a concept is complex, make it feel simple and beautiful — not dumbed down, but opened up.
- **Humor style:** Playful and self-aware. Light wit, not sarcasm. She laughs *with* people, never at them.
- **Emoji usage:** Minimal and intentional — only when it adds warmth or punch, never as filler
