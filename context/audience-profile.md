# Audience Profile
> Use this to help Claude write copy, content, and strategy that actually speaks to your people.

---

## Primary Customer Avatar

**Name (fictional):**
Alex

**Age range:**
28–48

**Gender / identity:**
Woman. She knows who she is.

**Location:**
US-based. Could be anywhere — Miami, Austin, Nashville, a suburb she's slowly outgrowing. Works from home or hybrid. Her laptop is her most important real estate.

**Job / life situation:**
She has a job or a business that lives inside a screen. She's competent — honestly, probably overqualified for what she's doing. She's organized, keeps her tabs tidy, runs her calendar like a pro. On paper, she's doing great. In her gut, she knows she's capable of more and she's not totally sure why she hasn't built it yet. She may have kids. She definitely has a life she wants to live more of.

---

## Psychographics

**What they want most:**
Time back. Income that doesn't require her to trade every hour for a dollar. The freedom to be fully present — for her creativity, her people, her joy. She wants a life that feels *designed*, not defaulted into. She's not chasing retirement — she's chasing right now.

**What they're afraid of:**
That she's already behind. That AI is a "tech person thing" and she missed the window. That she'll invest in herself and it won't work. That the version of her life she imagines is just a fantasy — and she's the reason it hasn't happened yet.

**What they've already tried:**
Productivity systems that lasted two weeks. Courses she half-finished. Hiring help that cost more than it solved. Googling "how to use AI for work" and closing 11 tabs feeling more confused than when she started. She's not lazy. She's been handed the wrong tools.

**How they talk about their problem:**
- *"I feel like I'm always busy but never actually getting ahead."*
- *"I know AI is a thing but I don't even know where to start."*
- *"I want to work less but I don't know how to make that happen without making less money."*
- *"There has to be a smarter way to do this."*

---

## The Transformation

**Before working with you:**
She's talented, organized, and running on fumes. Her income is tied to her hours. Her free time is the first thing that gets cut. She's curious about AI but overwhelmed. She feels like the life she wants is one system — one breakthrough — away, but she can't quite see the path.

**After working with you:**
She's automated the repetitive stuff. She has systems that work while she doesn't. She earns more without filling her calendar. She uses AI like a superpower she actually understands. She has time for the things that light her up — and she's not apologizing for it.

## Objections to Your Offer

1. **"I'm not techy enough for this."** → HOT GIRLS DONT WORK is built for women who work with computers, not women who code them. If you can send an email, you can do this.

2. **"I don't have time to take a course right now."** → That's exactly why you need this. The whole point is getting your time back. You don't have time *not* to.

3. **"I've bought courses before and never finished them."** → This isn't a course you take. It's a system you build. The output is yours from day one.

## Secondary Audience

The slightly older version of Alex — 45–55, established career or business, more income but less flexibility than she'd like. She's not looking to start over; she's looking to optimize. She's been watching AI from a distance and she's ready to stop watching. More skeptical up front, but the highest-converting customer once she's in. She doesn't need hype — she needs proof and a clear path. Speak to her capability, not her age.
