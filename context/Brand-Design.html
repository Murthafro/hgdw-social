Brand Gradient

The gradient runs left to right — warm yellow (#FFD56B) to coral (#FF7E5F) to deep sunset pink (#FF5263). In square/stacked layouts it runs diagonally top-left to bottom-right. The direction always tracks warm-to-pink. Never reversed.

#FFD56B — Yellow (start) → #FF7E5F — Coral (mid) → #FF5263 — Sunset (end)
Gradient CSS — Horizontal (primary):
linear-gradient(90deg, #FFD56B 0%, #FF7E5F 50%, #FF5263 100%)

Gradient CSS — Diagonal (square/stacked):
linear-gradient(135deg, #FFD56B 0%, #FF7E5F 50%, #FF5263 100%)

Direction always warm-to-pink. Never reverse to pink-to-warm.

Clear Space Rule

X = cap-height of the 'H' in the wordmark on all sides. Nothing intrudes into this zone.

Layout Principles

PrincipleWhat It MeansIn Practice
Hierarchy FirstEvery layout has one clear focal pointUse size, weight, and gradient to lead the eye
Warmth as TextureThe gradient is not decoration — it carries emotional weightUse it at key moments: headlines, borders, CTAs
Breathing RoomGenerous margins > cramped informationWhen in doubt, increase the margin
RhythmRepeat spacing patterns consistentlySame padding on same-type components, always
AlignmentEverything aligns to an invisible gridNothing should ever look 'eyeballed'
One MessageOne message per layout, one call to actionIf it requires a paragraph to explain, simplify the design
White Space Philosophy: White space is not wasted space. It is power. HGDW designs breathe — we never cram, we never rush. Generous margins and spacing signal luxury, confidence, and clarity. When in doubt, add more air.

The 10 Commandments

HOT GIRLS DONT WORK — BRAND COMMANDMENTS
  1. 1. The brand name is always HOT GIRLS DONT WORK. No apostrophe in DONT. Ever.
  2. 2. The logo is always set in Albert Sans Bold, ALL CAPS. Never in another font.
  3. 3. The gradient (#FFD56B → #FF7E5F → #FF5263) always moves warm-to-pink. Never reversed.
  4. 4. The gradient is the accent — 10% rule. It accents. It does not dominate.
  5. 5. Albert Sans is the only typeface. One font, applied with intention.
  6. 6. Headings are ALL CAPS. Body copy is sentence case. Always.
  7. 7. Photography is warm, editorial, and alive — not hustle, not stock, not stress.
  8. 8. Every design has one focal point, one message, one call to action.
  9. 9. White space is power. Never cram. Always breathe.
  10. 10. Joy is the strategy. If the design doesn't feel alive, it is not done.
